Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need technical skills?

No. Your site is ready with pages, colours and sample content. You change text, photos and settings from the Church Admin dashboard.

Who receives the tithes and partnership payments?

Your church does. You connect your own Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe or PayPal account, so money goes straight to your church account.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on Growth and Ministry. Add your domain in Church Admin → Custom domain, follow the DNS steps, and we connect it with free https.

What happens after the free trial?

Choose a plan and pay by card, bank transfer or USSD. If you do not, your site stays online for a short grace period and is then paused — your content is kept safe.
